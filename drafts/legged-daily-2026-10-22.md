### TactileStep: Sole Tactile Learning for Regulating Foot-Terrain Interaction in Humanoid Locomotion
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28959
- Source: arXiv
- Date: 2026-09-24
- Topics: humanoid locomotion / tactile sensing / sim-to-real / contact-phase rewards
- Summary: A deployable tactile learning framework that adds a thin pressure-sensing insole under each foot, aligns tactile simulation with the real sensor, and uses tactile and motion cues to identify contact phases for phase-aware rewards favoring softer landings and steadier support.
- Notes: Evaluated on Unitree G1 in simulation and hardware, reporting up to 48.8% lower peak touchdown force, up to 30.1 dB lower peak A-weighted impact noise, and up to 23.8% larger stance contact area over a strong perceptive baseline. The authors state the simulator assumes rigid foot–terrain contact (deformable/granular surfaces unvalidated) and that sensor durability and drift were not evaluated. A secondary news summary claims CoRL 2026 acceptance and a promised, not yet public, code release; neither was confirmed from the paper. Author list not verified in this run. Related: Tac4Loco (arXiv 2608.15766).

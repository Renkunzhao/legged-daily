# Legged Daily - 2026-06-19

## Summary
- SWAP is the strongest locomotion/control signal today: an equivariant symmetric world model for quadruped parkour, reporting real-world 2.13 m gap jumping and 1.63 m platform climbing.
- CTS-MoE is a practical perceptive locomotion update: perception-conditioned MoE routing plus task-specific critics for Unitree Go1 terrain adaptation across stairs, gaps, slopes, and transitions.
- HALOMI pushes humanoid loco-manipulation from scalable human demonstrations, with active perception and head-hand tracking validated on Unitree G1 real-world tasks.
- HumanoidArena adds a benchmark/project signal for egocentric hierarchical whole-body humanoid learning; the code repository appears not yet public, but the project page and page repo are live.
- EPFL BioRob has an active Fall 2026 Postdoc + PhD opening on neuromechanics of human locomotion and bio-inspired humanoid controllers.

<summary><strong>New Papers</strong></summary>

### SWAP: Symmetric Equivariant World-Model for Agile Robot Parkour
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19928
- Source: arXiv
- Date: 2026-06-18
- Authors: Kaixin Lan, Ze Wang, Hongyi Li, Lei Jiang, Chaojie Fu, Chengkai Su, Choi Lam Wong, Yongbin Jin, Hongtao Wang
- Topics: quadruped, agile locomotion, parkour, world models, equivariance, reinforcement learning
- Summary: Proposes an end-to-end symmetry-equivariant latent world model and actor-critic structure for quadruped parkour, using left-right symmetry as a structural prior to improve sample efficiency and geometric generalization.
- Notes: The abstract reports real-world record-style results of a robot leaping across a 2.13 m gap and climbing a 1.63 m platform, with zero-shot transfer across outdoor terrains.

### CTS-MoE: Implicit Terrain Adaptation via Mixture-of-Experts for Perceptive Locomotion
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19633
- Source: arXiv
- Date: 2026-06-17
- Authors: Francisco Affonso, Matheus P. Angarola, Ana Luiza Mineiro, Aditya Potnis, Marcelo Becker, Girish Chowdhary
- Topics: quadruped, perceptive locomotion, reinforcement learning, mixture-of-experts, terrain adaptation, sim-to-real
- Summary: Combines a dense MoE actor, perception-conditioned routing, and task-specific critic heads in a concurrent teacher-student RL setup so a Unitree Go1 can adapt to discontinuous terrain without explicit task labels at deployment.
- Notes: Project page reports hardware tests on seen and unseen terrain plus simulation gains, including a 29.3 percentage-point success-rate increase on gaps over a perceptive baseline.

### HALOMI: Learning Humanoid Loco-Manipulation with Active Perception from Human Demonstrations
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18772
- Source: arXiv
- Date: 2026-06-17
- Authors: Zehui Zhao, Yuxuan Zhao, Gaojing Zhang, Chenxi Liu, Maolin Zheng, Wenzhao Lian
- Topics: humanoid, loco-manipulation, active perception, imitation learning, human demonstrations, Unitree G1
- Summary: Introduces a scalable humanoid loco-manipulation framework that collects ego-view and wrist-view human demonstrations, aligns observations/actions, and uses a manifold-constrained head-hand tracking controller for robust transfer to a Unitree G1.
- Notes: Reports an 85% average success rate across three quantitatively evaluated real-world tasks, with qualitative demos including dynamic tossing and deep-squat grasping.

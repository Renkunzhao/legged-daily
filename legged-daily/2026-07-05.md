### HEFT: Heavy-Payload Full-size Humanoid Teleoperation with Privileged Motion Guidance and Windowed Payload Curriculum
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02332
- Source: arXiv
- Date: 2026-07-02
- Authors: Chenxin Liu, Qingzhou Lu, Guangxiao Yang, Xuanyang Shi, Chenghan Yang, Yanjiang Guo, Jianyu Chen
- Topics: humanoid teleoperation, heavy payload, motion tracking, VR teleoperation, curriculum learning, full-size humanoid
- Summary: Presents HEFT, a full-size humanoid teleoperation framework using Privileged Motion Guidance and a Windowed Payload Curriculum to track noisy VR references while carrying heavy payloads; deployment is reported on a 175 cm, 65 kg L7 humanoid with payloads up to 24 kg.
- Notes: Project page listed on arXiv: https://heft.axell.top/. Strong candidate for the master paper list because it focuses on payload-capable full-size humanoid teleoperation rather than only compact humanoids or payload-free motion tracking.

### Actuator Reality Shaping for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Robot Learning
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02205
- Source: arXiv
- Date: 2026-07-02
- Authors: Satoshi Yamamori, Koji Ishihara, Kentaro Minamikawa, Kiyoharu Ohmori, Taiyo Yazaki, Norikazu Sugimoto, Jun Morimoto
- Topics: sim-to-real, actuator dynamics, robot learning, zero-shot transfer, wheeled-legged robots, humanoid walking
- Summary: Proposes shaping physical actuator behavior to match the ideal second-order actuator model used in simulation, using a two-degree-of-freedom feedforward-feedback controller so policies can transfer zero-shot without task-level fine-tuning or learned actuator models.
- Notes: Broader robot-learning paper, but relevant here because the abstract explicitly reports zero-shot demonstrations on a wheeled-legged robot over a slope and a humanoid walking task.

### Multi-Rate Nonlinear Model Predictive Control for Wall-Supported Bipedal Locomotion of Quadrupedal Robots
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01574
- Source: arXiv
- Date: 2026-07-02
- Authors: Taizoon Chunawala, Jeeseop Kim, Kaveh Akbari Hamed
- Topics: quadruped locomotion, bipedal locomotion, wall-supported locomotion, nonlinear MPC, whole-body control, Unitree A1
- Summary: Introduces a layered MR-NMPC plus nonlinear whole-body control framework for wall-assisted bipedal locomotion on quadrupeds in constrained environments, planning contact points and continuous CoM/orientation trajectories within a multi-rate optimal-control formulation.
- Notes: arXiv comments say accepted to IEEE/RSJ IROS 2026; simulations use a Unitree A1 and report a 2.9x higher success rate than conventional MPC with heuristic foot placement.
